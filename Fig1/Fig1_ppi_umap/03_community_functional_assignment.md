## Claude's Functional Assignments

### Community 0 — Actin cytoskeleton remodeling & endocytic trafficking

Dominated by Rho GTPase regulators (ARHGAP, ARHGEF, DOCK, VAV3), actin/myosin machinery (MYH9, MYO family, VASP, WASF2, WIPF1), and endocytic/vesicle trafficking components (EPS15, PICALM, SNX family, SYNJ2, NUMB, CBL). This looks like the macrophage cytoskeletal response to signaling — phagocytosis, membrane ruffling, and receptor internalization machinery. Very consistent with macrophage activation states.

### Community 1 — Chromatin remodeling & transcriptional regulation

Heavily enriched for chromatin modifiers (EZH2, KDM family, KMT2A/D, SETD1A, SETD2, HDAC2/5, DOT1L), the SWI/SNF complex (SMARCA2/4, ARID1A, PBRM1), and transcriptional co-regulators (NCOA3/6, NCOR1, MED1/7, TAF1). Also contains the TFs ARID3A and ETV3. This is the epigenetic and transcriptional reprogramming community — likely reflecting the gene regulatory response downstream of IL6/IL10 signaling.

### Community 2 — RNA processing & post-transcriptional regulation

Almost entirely RNA biology — splicing factors (SRSF family, SF3B2, U2AF), RNA binding proteins (HNRNP family, RBM family, ZFP36 family), mRNA decay (AGO2, ZFC3H1, UPF2), and nuclear export (THOC5, TPR). ZFP36/ZFP36L1/L2 are particularly interesting here, as they are AU-rich element binding proteins that destabilize cytokine mRNAs — directly relevant to IL6/IL10 post-transcriptional control.

### Community 3 — Cytokine signaling & immune receptor response

This is the canonical macrophage immune signaling community. Contains STAT3, STAT6, NFATC1/2, NFKB2, IRF5, JUN, MEF2A/D as TFs, alongside receptor-proximal signaling (PTPN11, GAB1/2, SOS1/2, MAPK3), pattern recognition (NLRC4, NLRP1A, TICAM2), and immune receptors (IFNAR1, IFNGR1, PTPRC, CD44). This is the front-line cytokine response module — exactly what would be expected to be central in an IL6/IL10 BMDM system.

### Community 4 — mTOR/AMPK nutrient sensing & autophagy

Strikingly coherent — the mTORC1/2 complex (MTOR, RPTOR, RICTOR, MAPKAP1), upstream regulators (TSC1/2, PRKAA1, RRAGC/D, AKT1S1, PDPK1), autophagy machinery (ULK1/2, ATG16L2, ATG2B, RB1CC1, WIPI2, UVRAG, RUBCN), and the TFs TFE3 and TFEB, which are master regulators of lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy transcription. Also contains FOXO3/4 and CRTC1/2/3. This community is the metabolic and autophagic reprogramming arm of macrophage activation — highly relevant to IL6/IL10 since both cytokines regulate mTOR and macrophage metabolic state.
